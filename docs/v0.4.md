# v0.4 — Spectral Engine
Independent stereo post-rack module, FFT 1024 / hop 256, Hann windows and overlap-add. Freeze latches a non-silent frame with phase propagation; Blur diffuses frequency magnitudes and temporal memory; Stretch slows traversal of a 64-frame rolling history with periodic re-anchoring (not offline sample duration conversion); Morph interpolates magnitude and phase advance of two manually captured frames. One mode at a time. Wet/dry, mode and four amounts saved in APVTS; captures are transient session data, not embedded in state. The direct path is immediate; wet path has 1024 samples of creative-effect delay, not host-compensated. Off is bit-exact after fade; existing presets default to Off. Freeze and Morph can sustain after note-off until released or bypassed. No new scrollbars. Tests added for bypass, freeze, blur, stretch output, morph endpoints at four sample rates. Native CI and listening verification required.
