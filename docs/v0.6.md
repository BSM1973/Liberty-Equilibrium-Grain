# v0.6 Expressive Performance
Channel-aware direct MIDI, fixed MPE lower zone (channel 1 master, channels 2–16 members), configurable normal and member bend ranges, per-note poly pressure and channel pressure, CC74 to grain position, CC64 sustain, mono/legato last-note priority, glide (milliseconds per octave). Sequencer/arp still merge MIDI channels and do not preserve individual MPE or sustain gestures. MIDI Learn on rotary and modulation sliders, channel-specific CC, one target per CC and one CC per target, message-thread parameter notifications at 60 Hz (not sample accurate); CC64 and MPE CC74 reserved. Maps saved by parameter ID. Eight macros can individually replace their original assignment with a selectable single destination and bipolar depth; original assignments remain defaults. Morph A/B stores normalized numeric sound parameters including mod/FX/pattern settings, interpolates continuous values and switches discrete choices at midpoint. Excludes external sample files/paths, transient spectral captures, MIDI mappings, voice/MPE settings and macro assignment config. Capture and recall stored in project state; no dual-engine crossfade. Defaults preserve previous sound; native build, UI and CPU verification pending.
